## Live API facts (verified 2026-10-30, fixtures in Tests/TransportCatTests/Fixtures)
- `GET transit/linies/bus` (fixture `bus-lines-sample.json`, 13 of 115 lines, geometry trimmed): FeatureCollection; properties `CODI_LINIA` (Int, e.g. 212), `NOM_LINIA` ("H12"), `DESC_LINIA`, `ORIGEN_LINIA`, `DESTI_LINIA`, `NOM_FAMILIA` ("Horitzontals"), `CODI_FAMILIA`, `ORDRE_FAMILIA`, `ORDRE_LINIA`, `COLOR_LINIA` ("003888"), `COLOR_TEXT_LINIA` ("FFFFFF"), `NOM_OPERADOR`. Families: Proximitat 44, Convencionals 30, Verticals 17, Horitzontals 8, Diagonals 3, Llançadores 4, Bus a la Demanda 4, XPRESBus 3, BusTuristic 2.
- `GET transit/linies/bus/{code}/parades` (fixture `bus-stops-212.json`): all stops of both directions in one list (H12: 68 = 34 + 34). Properties: `CODI_PARADA`, `NOM_PARADA`, `ADRECA`, `ORDRE` (order inside its direction), `ID_SENTIT` (1 = Anada, 2 = Tornada), `DESC_SENTIT`, `ORIGEN_SENTIT`, `DESTI_SENTIT`, `DISTANCIA_PAR_ANTERIOR` (meters from the previous stop along the route), `ID_RECORREGUT` (route id), `CODI_INTERC`; geometry Point [lon, lat].
- `GET transit/linies/bus/{code}/recs` (fixture `bus-recs-212.json`): route geometries (4 features: day type and direction variants), properties `ID_RECORREGUT`, `ID_SENTIT`, `NOM_TIPUS_DIA`; geometry MultiLineString. A direction's geometry = the feature whose `ID_RECORREGUT` equals its stops' `ID_RECORREGUT`; fall back to the feature with the same `ID_SENTIT` having the most points.
- `GET itransit/bus/parades/{stop}` (one stop per call, 0.16 s): `{timestamp, parades:[{codi_parada, linies_trajectes:[{codi_linia, nom_linia, id_sentit, codi_trajecte, desti_trajecte, propers_busos:[{temps_arribada (epoch ms), id_bus (Int, may be null), info_bus:{accessibilitat:{estat_rampa}}}]}]}]}`; `estat_rampa` seen: "SENSE_INCIDENCIA". Fixture `ibus-H12-snapshot.json` = `{captured_at, stops:{<stopCode>:{timestamp, linies_trajectes:[...only H12...]}}}` for all 66 polled stops (131 arrivals, 17 distinct buses). Seconds = `(temps_arribada - timestamp) / 1000`, clamped at 0.
- `GET alerts/bus/channels/WEB` (fixture `alerts-bus.json`, 6 of 56 alerts): `data.alerts[]` with `id`, `begin`, `end` (epoch ms), `typeName` ("Parada anul·lada amb parada alternativa nova", "Modificació del recorregut ..."), `causeName`, `categories.messageType` (ALTERATION | SERVICE_MESSAGE), `channelInfoTO.textCa`, `linesAffected:[{commercialLineId ("H12"), lineId ("212"), ways:[{wayName, stops:[{stopId, stopName, nearStop, nearStopCode}]}]}]`, `stopsAffected`.
- A bus is placed from arrivals only; no GPS. Verified: polling a quarter of H12's stops already shows 12 distinct `id_bus`.

## Specs
- S1 Mode. `enum AppMode: String { case metro, bus }` persisted with `@AppStorage("appMode")`. A `ModeSelector` (chips "Metro" and "Bus", white-on-black selected style as in the prototype) sits at the top of the Mapa and Línies (Xarxa) tabs; the choice switches the whole screen between the existing metro screens and the new bus screens. The Xarxa tab is renamed "Línies" (same icon).
- S2 Bus catalog. Lines load once per launch (`busLines()`), grouped by family in the order `ORDRE_FAMILIA`, then `ORDRE_LINIA`. The picker sheet (opened from the line selector button on the bus map and on the bus Línies screen) has a search field (matches name, description, origin and destination, case- and diacritic-insensitive), a "Preferides" row (star toggle per line, persisted in `UserDefaults` as an array of line codes), family chips with counts and the line rows (badge, description, family). The last selected line persists.
- S3 Bus map. Shows the selected line: route polyline of each direction in the line color (the direction of the selected `Anada`/`Tornada` chip at full opacity, the other one at 40 %), stop dots (only when the visible latitude span is below 0.03 degrees), buses as pills with the line name in the line color with a heading notch (rotation `heading - 90 - cameraHeading`, like the metro train markers), the destination label only when zoomed in (span < 0.05), and the camera fitted to the line's route on line change. Buses of the other direction are drawn at 55 % opacity. Chips under the header: freshness ("En directe · fa 8 s", from the bus poll), incidents chip (bus alerts of the line), and a data-source strip "Posició estimada a partir de les arribades a N de M parades (TMB iBus). K busos seguits."
- S4 Bus positions. Per direction, an engine built on the POLLED stops (a subset, see S9) and the direction's route geometry reuses `TrainPositionEngine` with speed 4.5 m/s: stops become `Station` values (`BusStop.asStation(lineCode:)`), bus arrivals become `Arrival` values (`serviceCode = String(busID)`, `trajectCode = codi_trajecte`, `via = 1`: every bus moves toward the last stop of its own direction). Buses without `id_bus` are ignored. Smoothing uses `TrainMotion` (one instance per direction), ticker 10 Hz like `LineState`. A bus is on screen for the direction its arrivals belong to.
- S5 Poll budget. Per poll of the selected line: at most 30 stop requests (both directions together), always including the first and last polled stop of each direction and the rest evenly spaced; at most 6 requests in flight; interval 20 s; polling runs only while a bus screen of that line is visible (the view's `.task`). A failed stop request does not fail the poll; a poll with every request failed counts as failed. Freshness reuses `Freshness`.
- S6 Stop sheet. Tapping a stop dot opens a sheet (detents medium and large) for that stop: name, "Parada N · address", the stop's incidents (S7), and "Propers busos": every bus arrival at that stop from `busArrivals(stopCode:)` (all lines), sorted by seconds: line badge, destination, "bus <id>", ramp state (operational: green icon and "rampa operativa"; any other known state: orange "rampa amb incidència"; unknown/nil: nothing), countdown. Refreshes every 15 s while open; footer "Actualitzat fa N s · TMB iBus". Loading and error states as the metro station sheet.
- S7 Bus incidents. `BusAlertParser` maps bus alerts to `ServiceAlert` (`lineCodes` = every `lineId` as a string, `stationCodes` = every affected `stopId`, `stationNames` = stop names, `text` = `textCa`, dates from `begin`/`end`, `typeName` kept, `effectType` = `categories.messageType`). `AppModel` holds `busAlerts` and `busAlertsUpdatedAt`, loaded by `startAlertsPolling()` together with the metro alerts (same 60 s cycle) and exposes `activeBusAlerts(affecting lineCode: String, now: Date)`. `IncidentKind` gains `stopClosed` ("Parada anul·lada", typeName contains "anul·lada") and `detour` ("Recorregut modificat", typeName contains "recorregut"); other bus alerts are `other`. `IncidentIndex` is reused with `BusStop.asStation`. Severity stays facility (orange) for bus alerts.
- S8 Bus Línies (Xarxa). The bus Línies screen shows, for the selected line and direction (segmented control "Anada · <destination>" / "Tornada · <destination>"), the stops as rows on a spine in the line color with a stop marker, name, an arrival pill with the soonest bus countdown at that stop (from the last poll; only polled stops have one), buses drawn on the spine at a continuous position (`direction.stopIndexPosition(atRouteDistance:)`), stop incidents (orange rows with the kind icon and title, as in metro Xarxa) and the same chips and summary banner. The pill text color follows `HexLuminance` against the line color.
- S9 Freshness and honesty. Chips and strip as in S3; when the line has no bus in the feed the screen says "Cap bus en servei ara" instead of an empty map or list.
- S10 No regression. Metro screens, tests and behavior unchanged except the mode selector and the tab rename; existing tests keep passing.

Out of scope: FGC, planner, favorites sync, offline cache, nearby stops, per-stop timetables.
