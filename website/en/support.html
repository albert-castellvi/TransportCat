Frequently asked questions

Where do the arrivals come from?

From the TMB and FGC open data portals (CC BY 4.0), queried live from your phone. If TMB or FGC have an issue with their sources, the app shows it as best it can, but real time depends on the original service.

I see no trains on the line, but I know there is service

Sometimes the official source doesn't publish positions for a specific line or stretch (for example, the theoretical fleet at some point in the day). The app says so rather than inventing trains. Try reopening the map or switching lines and back.

Nearby stops don't show up

It needs the location permission: open iPhone Settings › TransportCat › Location and choose «While using the app».

Can I change the app's language?

Yes: the language is chosen in iPhone Settings › TransportCat › Language, with Catalan, Spanish and English available.

What about Rodalies?

That's what's being built right now: official timetables published daily and real-time for Rodalies de Catalunya will arrive in an upcoming update.
