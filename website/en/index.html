The whole network, alive

Open the app and the entire city is in front of you: every line drawn in its real colour, trains and buses moving second by second, incidents marked where they happen.

Five modes: All, Metro, Bus, FGC and Rodalies

Switch modes with a tap and the map turns from one to the other. All paints everything; the starting mode is picked in Settings.

Live vehicle positions

Trains and buses move along their tracks second by second, with the live badge telling you how long ago the network answered.

Your lines, always at hand

Pick a line in the bottom switcher and the map adjusts: track, stations, vehicles and alerts for that line in one view.

Arrivals, to the second

At every stop and station, exact minutes for each destination — and a green «now» when the vehicle is pulling in. No reloading, no waiting: the screen stays live on its own.

  • Real-time metro and bus arrivals
  • Station sheet with departures and delays
  • Both directions, two destinations, one glance

FGC trains, with GPS

FGC trains ride along their real tracks with live GPS positions. Tap a station and its sheet tells you which trains are there right now, where they're headed and whether they're on time.

  • Trains moving along the map
  • On-time status for every train
  • Follow a train and the map follows it

Plan a trip

Point A to point B in a single screen: pick your origin and destination, decide whether you leave now or later, and let the app take you there.

Explore, search, save

Search any station, stop or line from the magnifier. Save favourites and order them your way. And in Nearby, the arrivals of the stops around you, sorted by distance.

  • Search stations, stops and lines
  • Favourites in your own order
  • Nearby: what's happening at your stops

Service alerts, before you leave home

Engineering works, delays and diverted lines, explained plainly with the affected stations. Turn on alerts for your lines and they arrive as notifications.

  • Metro and bus alerts in one place
  • Notifications for your lines
  • Date ranges and affected stations

Your arrival, on the Lock Screen

When you know which train or bus you're catching, the live countdown lives on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. And if you want a heads-up for one arrival, a reminder lets you know before it gets there.

Yours, from the first minute

Choose the map's starting mode, order your favourites, turn on alerts for your lines and check your location and notification permissions — all in one Settings screen. The app speaks Catalan, Spanish and English.

  • Choose the map's starting mode
  • Catalan, Spanish and English
  • Permissions in the open, no surprises

Now with Rodalies

Rodalies de Catalunya trains now have a home in TransportCat: the chosen line on the map with its trains live and Renfe's GPS positions, the stations in order in Stops, and every station's next departures with the real-time delays.

  • 19 lines in the Catalonia nucleus
  • Trains on the move under the live badge
  • Renfe's official timetables, published daily

Your data stays on your phone

TransportCat has no account, doesn't track you and collects no personal data. Location is only used on the device to find nearby stops. Network data comes from the TMB, FGC and Renfe open data portals (CC BY 4.0).
