Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary

TransportCat asks for no account and does not track you. There is no advertising, no profiling, and we sell your data to no one. Below we explain exactly what leaves your phone, when, and where it goes.

What the app does with your data

  • Location. Used while the app is open to find nearby stops and calculate distances, which happens on the device. If you plan a trip from your location, the coordinates are sent to TMB, which receives them to calculate the route; TransportCat does not store them on any server of its own. You can revoke the permission at any time from iOS Settings.
  • Favourites and reminders. Stored locally on the device and, if you use the widget, shared with the widget through the system's shared storage. If you have an Apple Watch, they sync to your watch: they move between your own devices, never through any server. None of this data reaches us or any third party.
  • Notifications. Arrival reminders and service alerts are scheduled on the device. No server ever notifies you: everything is generated by the phone.
  • Diagnostics. On an error or crash, the app may send technical diagnostics (crash traces and performance metrics) through Sentry. These reports carry no name or email of yours and are handled pseudonymously; they exist only to fix bugs.

If you write to us

From Settings → «Write to us» you can send us a free-text message. The email field is optional: fill it in only if you want a reply, and we use it only to answer you. Messages arrive at Sentry (sentry.io), the same service that collects the diagnostics described above, so we can read and answer them; if the message contains personal data, it is only what you type into it. If you want a message you sent deleted, write to the contact address in the footer and we will do it.

Where the transport data comes from

Real-time data (arrivals, line geometries, alerts) comes from TMB (through its developer portal, under its terms of use) and from the open data portals of FGC and Renfe (licensed CC BY 4.0). The app queries them directly from the phone; there is no intermediate server of ours.

The preprocessed timetables the app relies on for some services are downloaded from this project's public GitHub releases. That means GitHub (github.com) receives your IP address whenever the app downloads a timetable.

TransportCat is not an official TMB, FGC or Renfe application and has no affiliation with them.

Third parties

Three third parties are involved: Sentry (sentry.io), the error diagnostics and «Write to us» message service described above; GitHub (github.com), where the app downloads the preprocessed timetables described above; and TMB, which receives the coordinates when you plan a trip from your location. There are no advertising third parties, no third-party analytics and no other integrations.

Your rights

If you wrote to us through the form (with or without an email), you can ask us to access, correct or delete what you sent: write to the contact address and we will take care of it. The rest of the data (favourites, reminders) lives only on your device and we cannot see it: if you uninstall the app, everything is gone from the phone.

Contact

Any question about this policy: support@transportcat.app
