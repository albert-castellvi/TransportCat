Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Summary

TransportCat asks for no account, does not track you and collects no personal data. There is no advertising, no profiling, and we sell or share nothing — because there is nothing to sell or share.

What the app does with your data

  • Location. Used only on the device, while the app is open, to find nearby stops and calculate distances. It never leaves the phone. You can revoke the permission at any time from iOS Settings.
  • Favourites and reminders. Stored locally on the device and, if you use the widget, shared with the widget through the system's shared storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Notifications. Arrival reminders and service alerts are scheduled on the device. No server ever notifies you: everything is generated by the phone.
  • Diagnostics. On an error or crash, the app may send anonymous technical diagnostics (crash traces and performance metrics) through Sentry. These reports contain no personal data, no location and no advertising identifiers, and exist only to fix bugs.

Where the transport data comes from

Arrivals, line geometries and alerts come from the open data portals of TMB and FGC, licensed CC BY 4.0. The app queries them directly from the phone; there is no intermediate server of ours.

TransportCat is not an official TMB or FGC application and has no affiliation with them.

Third parties

The only third party involved is Sentry (getsentry.com) as an error diagnostics service, as described above. There are no advertising third parties, no third-party analytics and no other integrations.

Your rights

Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to give you access to, correct or delete. If you uninstall the app, everything is gone: favourites, reminders and history all lived on the phone.
