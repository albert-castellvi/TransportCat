Prompt/design: A full-square warm off-white app icon with a compact schematic
network of five thick red, green, blue, yellow and purple routes. Straight segments,
rounded elbows, small white station dots and outlined interchange circles.
No vehicles, typography, T monogram, operator logos, outer mask or device mockup.
An original abstract emblem inspired by Barcelona metro cartography, not an
accurate navigable network diagram. Pending Xcode integration and archive validation.
